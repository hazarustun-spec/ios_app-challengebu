About the app:
  ChallengeBu is an independent community tennis ranking app for university players. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any university. Access is restricted to verified university email addresses purely as a functional sign-up gate (to keep the community to students/faculty/alumni) — this is access control, not a claim of institutional affiliation, and no university name, logo, or branding is used in the app or its metadata.

Pre-populated review data:
  The review account is seeded with a live, ranked leaderboard (9 players), completed match history and profile stats, an incoming pending challenge, and ONE match that is ready to score right now — so every feature can be exercised without waiting for other users. The reviewer can explore: leaderboard categories (Sıralama), profile stats + ELO history (Profil), notifications, settings (including account deletion), messaging with block/report, and the full match lifecycle below.
