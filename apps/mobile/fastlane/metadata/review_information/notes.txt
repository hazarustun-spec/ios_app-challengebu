ChallengeBu! uses email sign-in only — there is NO password field anywhere in the app.
There is nothing to enter in App Store Connect's "Sign-In Information" password
field; leave it blank or put "N/A" there.

Sign-in (App Review demo account):
  Email: appreview42@proton.me

Steps:
  1. Launch → tap "Üniversite e-postanla başla"
  2. Enter appreview42@proton.me
  3. Check KVKK consent → tap "Devam et"
  4. The account signs in immediately — no code, no password, no second screen.
     You land directly on the onboarding wizard (name, pronoun, category, year,
     department, level, hand, availability — phone and photo are optional/
     skippable) the first time, or straight on the main app (Sıralama, Maçlar,
     Profil tabs) on any later sign-in.

Note: real users sign in with a .edu.tr university e-mail address and enter a
6-digit code that is mailed to them. Only the review account skips that step:
since the reviewer can't read the appreview42@proton.me inbox, that one address
signs in on a single tap instead, so the review can proceed without a mailbox.
This is a review-only shortcut, not a change to what real users experience.

About the app:
  ChallengeBu! is an independent community tennis ranking app for university
  players. Sign-up is gated to e-mail addresses under the Turkish academic
  top-level .edu.tr — an access-control check to keep the community to
  students, faculty and alumni. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
  sponsored by, or a copy of any specific university; no university name,
  logo, colors, or branding is used in the app or its metadata.

Pre-populated review data:
  The review account is seeded with a live, ranked leaderboard (9 players),
  completed match history and profile stats, an incoming pending challenge,
  and ONE match that is ready to score right now — so every feature can be
  exercised without waiting for other users. The reviewer can explore:
  leaderboard categories (Sıralama), profile stats + ELO history (Profil),
  notifications, settings (including account deletion), messaging with
  block/report, and the full match lifecycle below.

Demonstrating the FULL match flow (start → score → result):
  1. Open the Maçlar (Matches) tab → the pre-seeded match card shows
     "Maçı Başlat".
  2. Tap "Maçı Başlat" to start (it is already within the allowed start window).
  3. Enter points to complete the set, then submit the score.
  (You can also tap the "+" to create a brand-new challenge against any seeded
  opponent, or accept the incoming pending challenge shown in notifications.)

Device notes:
  iPhone-first app (portrait). On iPad it runs in iPhone compatibility mode;
  core flows work the same.

Messaging (Guideline 1.2):
  In-thread ⋯ menu offers Report and Block. Blocked users can be managed under
  Settings → Engellenen kullanıcılar. Messaging requires an active match offer
  between two users.
