ChallengeBu! uses email sign-in only — there is NO password field anywhere in
the app. Leave the "Sign-In Information" password field blank or type "N/A".

Sign-in (App Review demo account):
  Email: appreview42@proton.me

Steps:
  1. Launch → tap "Üniversite e-postanla başla"
  2. Enter appreview42@proton.me
  3. Check the KVKK consent box → tap "Devam et"
  4. The account signs in on a single tap — no code, no password, no second
     screen. Every review sign-in resets the demo profile to an un-onboarded
     state, so you land on the full onboarding wizard (name, pronoun,
     category, class year, department, level, hand, availability — phone and
     photo are optional/skippable). Walk through the wizard once, then you
     reach the main app (Sıralama, Maçlar, Profil tabs).

Note: real users sign in with a .edu.tr university e-mail address and enter a
6-digit code that is mailed to them. Only the review account skips that step:
since the reviewer can't read the appreview42@proton.me inbox, that one
address signs in on a single tap instead, so the review can proceed without
a mailbox. This is a review-only shortcut, not a change to what real users
experience.

About the app:
  ChallengeBu! is an independent community tennis ranking app for university
  players. Sign-up is gated to e-mail addresses under the Turkish academic
  top-level .edu.tr — an access-control check to keep the community to
  students, faculty, and alumni. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed
  by, sponsored by, or a copy of any specific university; no university
  name, logo, colors, or branding is used in the app or its metadata.

Pre-populated review data:
  The demo account lands on a live, populated app after finishing the
  onboarding wizard:
    - A ranked leaderboard with 6 players (the demo account + 5 seeded
      opponents): 2 in Erkek Tek (men's singles), 2 in Kadın Tek (women's
      singles), everyone in Open Tek.
    - Completed match history on the demo profile (3 matches: 2 won, 1 lost)
      driving ELO history + form stats.
    - ONE match already ready to score right now — no waiting required.
    - ONE incoming pending challenge from a seeded opponent, so the accept
      flow is exercisable.

Demonstrating the FULL match flow (start → score → confirm):
  1. Open the Maçlar (Matches) tab → the pre-seeded card shows "Maçı
     Başlat".
  2. Tap "Maçı Başlat" to start (it is already within the allowed start
     window).
  3. Enter points to complete the set, then submit the score.
  You can also tap "+" to create a brand-new challenge against any seeded
  opponent, or accept the incoming pending challenge shown in notifications.

Device notes:
  iPhone-first app (portrait). On iPad it runs in iPhone compatibility mode;
  core flows work the same.

Messaging (Guideline 1.2):
  In-thread ⋯ menu offers Report and Block. Blocked users can be managed
  under Settings → Engellenen kullanıcılar. Messaging requires an active
  match offer between two users.
